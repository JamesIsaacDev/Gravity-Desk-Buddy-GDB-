\# GDB Orbit Architecture — Version 2



\## Purpose



The Orbit system is GDB's long-term developmental layer.



Daily focus, reflection, and Buddy engagement form the foundation. Orbits may eventually help users understand how their judgment, learning patterns, and applied thinking develop over time within a chosen domain.



\## Core Philosophy



GDB does not primarily ask:



> What do you do?



It asks:



> Who are you becoming, and what evidence demonstrates that development?



Orbits should represent earned development over time—not purchased status, self-declared intelligence, popularity, or one isolated test result.
