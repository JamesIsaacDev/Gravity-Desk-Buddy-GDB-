\# GDB Product Vision



\## Core Purpose



Gravity Desk Buddy helps people build evidence of personal and professional development through focus, reflection, consistency, and guided progression.



\## Year 1 Scope



\- Focus sessions

\- Buddy

\- Streaks

\- Journal and reflection

\- Basic Orbits



\## Orbit Home Experience



The Buddy sits at the emotional and visual centre of the interface.



Navigation options are arranged around the Buddy like a clock. Possible destinations include:



\- Messages

\- Connections

\- Settings

\- Development

\- Profile

\- Journal



The interface should feel colourful, personal, customizable, and alive rather than resembling a conventional top-navigation website.



\## Example Profile



James



Software Engineering

The profile may include both the user’s real profile picture and customized Buddy.



\## Product Principle



GDB does not primarily ask, “What do you do?”



GDB asks:



“Who are you becoming, and what evidence demonstrates that development?”

## Future Founder Operations



A future internal GDB command center may summarize:



\- Users

\- Revenue

\- Product health

\- Infrastructure

\- Support

\- Team progress



This is not part of the Year 1 MVP initially.
