\# Gravity Daily Buddy (GDB)



Gravity Daily Buddy is a focus and human-development platform designed to help people consistently act on who they are becoming.



\## The Core Problem



Distraction, inconsistency, and loss of direction prevent people from doing meaningful work and developing their capabilities over time.



\## MVP Foundation



The first version of GDB focuses on a simple daily loop:



1\. Choose meaningful work.

2\. Complete a focus session.

3\. Reflect through a journal prompt.

4\. Grow a streak.

5\. Strengthen the relationship with a personalized Buddy companion.



\## Long-Term Vision



GDB may eventually connect daily action with long-term development through:



\- Domain-based development journeys

\- Multiple thinking frameworks

\- AI-assisted reflection

\- Behaviour and learning trends

\- Earned Orbit recommendations

\- Cross-domain communities

\- Human review for advanced Orbit progression



GDB does not merely ask, “What do you do?”



It asks:



> \\\*\\\*Who are you becoming, and what evidence shows your development?\\\*\\\*



\## Orbit Philosophy



Orbits are intended to represent development over time—not purchased status, self-declared intelligence, or a single test result.
